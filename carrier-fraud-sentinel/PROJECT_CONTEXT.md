## Business Rules & Thresholds (LOCKED)

### Rule 1: Payment Reconciliation
- **Pattern:** Carrier accepts offer but never pays
- **Source:** WTRANSNET API (mock for demo)
- **Detection:** Compare accepted offers vs paid transports
- **Alert:** Unpaid rate > 80%

### Rule 2: Offer Price Escalation
- **Pattern:** Carrier sees cheaper offer, accepts inflated version later
- **Source:** WTRANSNET visibility logs (mock)
- **Detection:** Price increase > 20% between first view and acceptance
- **Alert:** Suspicious pricing pattern

### Rule 3: Complaint Accumulation (MOST CRITICAL)
- **5+ open cases simultaneously** = ALERT
- **10+ mediated cases in 1 week** = ALERT
- **20+ cases in 1 month** = ALERT
- **2+ accidents in 1 month** = ALERT
- **3+ cases closed as commercial dispute (unresolved)** = CRITICAL ALERT
