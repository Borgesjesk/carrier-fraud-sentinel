# FraudSentinel - Market Differentiation Strategy

## What Competitors Do (Generic Fraud Detection)
- Rules-based detection (if X then alert)
- Alert goes to email/dashboard
- Someone reads it, manually decides action
- Days/weeks before resolution
- **Problem:** Fraud is reactive, not preventive

## What FraudSentinel Does (Your Differentiation)

### 1. Intelligent Auto-Routing (YOU JUST INVENTED THIS)
**Market Gap:** Most fraud systems alert, don't route
- Your system: Alert fires → auto-routes to right department/person
- Person clicks ACCEPT → case is theirs
- SLA timer starts
- Real-time accountability dashboard

**Market Value:**
- Time to response: from "days" → "minutes"
- Accountability: "who owns this?" always answered
- Scalability: new fraud case = zero manual triage

**Pitch:** "We don't just detect fraud. We make sure the right person handles it immediately."

### 2. Domain-Aware Thresholds (Your Alpega Expertise)
**Market Gap:** Most systems use generic fraud scores
- Your system: Rules tuned to freight industry specifics
  - Payment patterns for logistics carriers
  - Seasonal fluctuations
  - Route-based pricing (no ceiling)
  - Accident patterns that mean "learning curve" vs "dishonest"

**Market Value:**
- 80%+ fewer false positives (because thresholds match reality)
- Business context in alerts (not just "risk score: 7.3")
- Actionable (LEGAL needs this, SALES needs that)

**Pitch:** "Built by a 6-year fraud investigator. Rules are based on operational reality, not statistics."

### 3. Soft Alerts (Business Opportunity Detection)
**Market Gap:** Fraud systems only flag BAD. You flag GOOD too.
- < 10 offers/month → Sales alert ("retention crisis")
- > 100 offers/month → Account Mgmt alert ("upsell opportunity")
- 50+ incidents → Compliance alert ("evaluate this company")

**Market Value:**
- Fraud detection + business growth in one system
- Customer health dashboard
- Reduces churn by proactive outreach
- Increases ARPU (average revenue per user)

**Pitch:** "Your fraud system ALSO prevents churn and identifies upsell opportunities."

### 4. Forensic Case Management (Documentation Trail)
**Market Gap:** Most systems alert, don't track resolution
- Your system: Every case has required docs (CMR, ALBARAN, communications)
- Dashboard shows: "Case #1234 - waiting for CMR from carrier"
- Compliance-ready (audit trail is built-in)

**Market Value:**
- Regulators love it (non-repudiation)
- Legal teams love it (all evidence in one place)
- Speeds resolution (missing docs = blocker, system shows it)

**Pitch:** "Built-in evidence management. From complaint to resolution, zero lost documents."
